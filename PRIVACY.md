# Privacy Policy

**LibreForecast** · Effective: November 25, 2026

LibreForecast is a free weather app. There is no account to make, no ads and no analytics, and nothing you do in it is sold or shared for marketing. This page lists the little it uses, where that goes, and how to remove it.

## What the app keeps

- **Your settings and recent places.** Units, clock format, map options and the places you looked up. Kept only on your phone.
- **Your location.** Only if you allow it, to show the weather where you are. Kept only on your phone.

There is no LibreForecast server and no copy of any of this anywhere else.

## Who else sees anything

To draw the map and the forecast, the app asks these services for data. Most of them only see the part of the map you are looking at.

| Service | What it gets |
| :--- | :--- |
| [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) | The coordinates of the place you are viewing, to send back its forecast. Place search also goes to Open-Meteo. |
| [BigDataCloud](https://www.bigdatacloud.com) | Your coordinates once per update, to look up the name of the town you are in. |
| [NASA GIBS](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov) | Requests for the satellite pictures and fire spots on the map. |
| [RainViewer](https://www.rainviewer.com) | Requests for the rain radar on the map. |
| [Esri](https://www.esri.com) | Requests for the base map and place names. |
| [GDACS](https://www.gdacs.org) | Nothing about you. The app only downloads the typhoon tracks it publishes. |

Like any website, these services can see your phone's IP address when it asks them for data. Each one handles that under its own privacy policy.

## What the app never does

- Ask for your name, email or a password.
- Show ads or read an advertising ID.
- Follow you across other apps or websites.
- Sell your data, or hand it to anyone for their own use.

## Keeping it safe

Everything the app downloads travels over HTTPS.

## Notifications

Weather and typhoon alerts are made on your phone from the forecast. Turning them off in Settings stops them.

## Removing your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. That deletes everything it kept.

## Children

LibreForecast is made for a general audience and is not aimed at children under 13.

## Support payments

This GitHub page links to Buy Me a Coffee. If you choose to support the app there, Buy Me a Coffee handles your payment and details under its own privacy policy. The app itself never sees them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here and ships with the app, and the date at the top changes with it.
